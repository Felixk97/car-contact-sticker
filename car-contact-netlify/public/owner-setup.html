🔔 Vehicle Owner Setup

One-time setup

  1. Install the free ntfy app on your phone.
  2. Pick a long, random topic name and subscribe to it in the app — this is your private notification channel.
  3. In Netlify, go to Site settings → Environment variables and add PLATE_TOPIC_MAP with a value like: 
    {"PNW2990":"your-topic-here","PKJ999":"your-topic-here"}
    Plate numbers as keys have NO spaces and are UPPERCASE. All your own cars can point to the same topic (your phone).
  4. Also add each new plate as an option in public/index.html (and visitor.html) — find the <select class="plate-select"> section and add a line like: 
    <option value="PKJ999">PKJ 999</option>
  5. Deploy the site, open it, and send yourself a test alert to confirm it reaches your phone.

Adding a car for a different owner later

Just add their plate to PLATE_TOPIC_MAP pointing at their topic instead of yours, and add the plate as a dropdown option. No code changes needed beyond that.

QR code

Since the page now has a dropdown, you only need ONE QR code for all your cars — it can point straight at your site's root URL. The visitor picks which car it's about after scanning.
